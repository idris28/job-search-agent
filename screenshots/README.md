# The Job Search website in pictures

One picture for each thing the website does, in the order you would meet them. They were taken on a demo copy with made-up jobs,
people, employers and addresses, so nothing here is real data, and nothing was sent or searched to make them.
[WEBSITE_GUIDE.md](../WEBSITE_GUIDE.md) explains each of these in words.

## The screen at a glance

### Shortlist: the home screen

The menu on the left, the six numbers across the top, the filter bar and table of shortlisted jobs, the pipeline and top matches on the right.

![Shortlist: the home screen](01-shortlist.png)

### The menu and its actions

The seven screens with their counts, then the one-click actions that work from any screen.

![The menu and its actions](02-menu-and-actions.png)

### The six numbers

How many jobs are at each stage. Clicking one opens those jobs. The line under Scored says how many still wait.

![The six numbers](03-numbers.png)

### Where the jobs are, and Next steps

Scored jobs by country, and what is worth doing next, each with its button.

![Where the jobs are, and Next steps](04-next-steps.png)

### Pipeline and Top matches

One mark per job on your lists, coloured by stage, and your best-scoring jobs.

![Pipeline and Top matches](05-pipeline.png)

### All actions (Cmd-K)

A searchable list of every action and screen.

![All actions (Cmd-K)](06-all-actions.png)

### All actions, searched

Type a few letters, then press Enter.

![All actions, searched](07-all-actions-search.png)

### The light look

Light and Dark at the bottom of the menu switch the look.

![The light look](08-light-look.png)

## Finding jobs

### The arrow beside Find new jobs

Search every site, collect only, score waiting jobs, the morning routine, or search one site.

![The arrow beside Find new jobs](09-find-new-jobs-menu.png)

### A search running

A line moves under the numbers it is working on, and the strip under them shows the latest message, with Show output and Stop.

![A search running](10-search-running.png)

### Activity while a task runs

Everything the task has reported so far, updating as it happens, above the list of past searches.

![Activity while a task runs](11-activity-running.png)

### Stopping a task

Stop asks first. What the task already saved is kept.

![Stopping a task](12-stop-a-task.png)

### Activity: the last task and past searches

The finished task's full output, and each past search with its result, counts, AI tokens and, when it was only partly finished, why.

![Activity: the last task and past searches](13-activity-finished.png)

### A finished task

The strip says done and the lists refresh by themselves.

![A finished task](14-search-finished.png)

### Add from a post

Paste a LinkedIn post, a WhatsApp message or an email. The role, company and address are read from it, and the job is scored and shortlisted.

![Add from a post](15-add-from-a-post.png)

## General applications: HR addresses

### Add HR addresses

One address per line, with the employer's name before it if you know it.

![Add HR addresses](16-add-hr-addresses.png)

### What was added and what was left out

Each address becomes a general application on the shortlist. A line with no address, or one already on your list, is listed with the reason and stays in the box.

![What was added and what was left out](17-add-hr-addresses-result.png)

### General applications on the shortlist

Choose General applications in the Next step filter. Each row shows the employer and the address.

![General applications on the shortlist](18-general-applications-shortlist.png)

## The table

### Filters

Country, score, how recently it was posted, and next step. The count beside the title says how many match.

![Filters](19-table-filters.png)

### Search jobs

Looks at the title, company, location and email address.

![Search jobs](20-table-search.png)

### Sorting

Click a column heading to sort, again to reverse, a third time to go back to newest first.

![Sorting](21-table-sorted.png)

### Pages

Eight jobs show per page. The buttons under the table move between pages.

![Pages](22-table-pages.png)

### When nothing matches

Clear the filters brings every job back.

![When nothing matches](23-table-nothing-matches.png)

## A job, opened

### A job, opened

Clicking a row slides in a panel from the right. Esc, the X or a click outside closes it; the arrows or J and K move to the next job.

![A job, opened](24-job-opened.png)

### Top of the panel

Title, company, place, age, pay and score. Apply on the job site opens the posting; the next buttons record what happened; Other status has the rest.

![Top of the panel](25-job-top.png)

### The email, ready to send

The email is a sheet of paper: To, Subject and the text, all editable. Save draft keeps your edits; Send email sends it after asking.

![The email, ready to send](26-job-email-ready.png)

### Preview

Preview shows the email as the recruiter will see it. Edit brings the text back.

![Preview](27-job-email-preview.png)

### Cover letter

For sites that ask for one. Copy puts it on the clipboard; Write it again makes a new one.

![Cover letter](28-job-cover-letter.png)

### Answers for the application form

Your own details and short answers written for this job, each with a Copy button, to paste into the site's form yourself. Nothing is submitted for you.

![Answers for the application form](29-job-application-answers.png)

### Notes, history and the full job description

Your own notes, every status change and email with its date, and the original posting text.

![Notes, history and the full job description](30-job-description.png)

### Send email asks first

The question names the address. Only Send email in this box sends it.

![Send email asks first](31-send-email-question.png)

### A job with no draft yet

Draft the email writes one from your template and resume.

![A job with no draft yet](32-job-no-draft.png)

### A draft with no address

The line under the buttons says why there is no Send email. Type an address and Save address, or Look for one.

![A draft with no address](33-job-no-address.png)

### A job you applied to

Once a job is applied, the buttons record what happened next: Got an interview, Rejected, and later Got an offer.

![A job you applied to](34-job-top-applied.png)

### An email that already went

The round mark is the day it was sent. Send again writes to the same address once more, after asking.

![An email that already went](35-job-email-sent.png)

### Send again asks first

It names the day the first email went and the address. Send ready emails never writes to the same job twice; only this does.

![Send again asks first](36-send-again-question.png)

### A follow-up that is due

The stored follow-up text sits under the email. I followed up myself records one you sent by hand.

![A follow-up that is due](37-job-follow-up-due.png)

### Notes and history

Notes are yours alone. History lists every status change and email with its date.

![Notes and history](38-job-notes-history.png)

### A message after an action

A short message at the bottom right confirms what was done, or says what went wrong and what to do.

![A message after an action](39-message.png)

### Leaving with unsaved changes

The page asks before it throws away something you typed.

![Leaving with unsaved changes](40-unsaved-changes-question.png)

### After Apply on the job site

The posting opens in a new tab. When you come back, the page asks whether you applied, and Yes moves the job to In progress.

![After Apply on the job site](41-did-you-apply.png)

### Delete entry

Only something you added yourself, and have not emailed, can be deleted. It asks first.

![Delete entry](42-delete-entry-question.png)

![Send ready emails](43-send-ready-emails-question.png)

### The Shortlist buttons

Draft emails, Look for addresses, Save drafts to Gmail and Send ready emails, each with how many jobs it applies to.

![The Shortlist buttons](44-shortlist-toolbar.png)

## Settings

### Settings, the whole screen

Setup, the morning routine, duplicate jobs, your templates, what is searched and the limits, the job sites, backups, and stopping the program.

![Settings, the whole screen](52-settings.png)

### Setup

A green dot for what is ready, red for what is missing, grey for something optional. Keys and passwords stay in the .env file and are never shown.

![Setup](53-settings-setup.png)

### Every morning

Pick a time and click Run every morning; the Mac then runs the morning routine daily while it is awake.

![Every morning](54-settings-every-morning.png)

### Duplicate jobs

If the same job is stored twice, Merge folds the copies together and keeps your score and status.

![Duplicate jobs](55-settings-duplicate-jobs.png)

### Your templates: Email

The wording used for every recruiter email. Text in [square brackets] is filled in for each job.

![Your templates: Email](56-settings-templates-email.png)

### Your templates: Cover letter

The buttons at the top right switch between the email, general email and cover letter templates.

![Your templates: Cover letter](57-settings-templates-cover-letter.png)

### Search and limits

What is searched for and the limits. A value that differs from config.yaml is marked changed; Go back to config.yaml drops your changes.

![Search and limits](58-settings-search-and-limits.png)

### Sites

A job board can be given its own browser, so Browserbase time goes only to the boards that refuse the browser on this Mac. Below, one switch per job site: a site that is off is skipped by every search.

![Sites](59-settings-sites.png)

### Backups

Back up now copies the whole database into the backups folder. The morning routine also makes one each day.

![Backups](60-settings-backups.png)

### Stop Job Search

Stops the program completely, after asking. Double-click Job Search to start it again.

![Stop Job Search](61-stop-job-search-question.png)
